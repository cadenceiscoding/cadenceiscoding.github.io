Memory Allocation

char *ptr = malloc(1000000* sizeof(char));

 

when I reminisce

my childhood is a collection of colourful feeling,

murky detail and misremembered words.

 

the happiest of my memories are faded,

replayed too many times,

a moment once lived

shrunken to echoes of sensation.

 

if (ptr == NULL) {

      return 1;

}

 

the sharpest moments come easily,

often unbidden,

guarding the space they hold with

snapping teeth and sorrow.

 

I fear its finity,

the capacity of my mind,

whether there is room for the future in my bursting brain

or if whole blocks of time will blend together,

condense my character with it,

make me unfamiliar in the merciless process.

 

free(ptr);
