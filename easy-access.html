ls -l -a me

 

I have long been warned to keep myself secret,

avoid the attention of the unknown,

hunch inward and

remain unthreatening. 

 

chmod g+rwx me

 

I open myself to you,

in naivety or boldness,

forgoing the shame of hiding

for the thrill of being prone.

 

I am eager to please,

inviting you in to take control,

mold the output

into something I could not achieve on my own.

 

touch me

 

I should fear a connection so revealing,

shudder at what you could do if desired,

or so I am told.

 

rm -f me
